# Privacy Policy for Rass Derb Cup Discord Bot

**Effective Date:** November 19, 2026  
**Last Updated:** November 19, 2026

Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how the **Rass Derb Cup Discord Bot** ("the Bot") collects, uses, and protects your information.

---

### 1. Information Collected
When interacting with the Bot, the following data may be processed:
* **Discord Identifiers:** User ID, username, display name, and avatar URL when submitting forms or interacting with commands.
* **Guild Information:** Server ID and channel IDs necessary to route embed announcements and form submissions.
* **Form Submissions:** Responses voluntarily submitted through the Bot's interactive modals, which are delivered directly to designated staff channels.

---

### 2. How Information is Used
* To route tournament and support form answers to the server's designated private staff rooms.
* To render custom embed announcements as requested by authorized server administrators.
* To maintain persistent interactive buttons across bot restarts.

We **do not** sell, trade, rent, or share personal user data with any third parties or advertisers.

---

### 3. Data Storage & Security
* Form definitions, section configurations, and button metadata are stored securely in the Bot's database.
* Answers submitted by users are transmitted to the server's staff channels and are not permanently archived in our database.

---

### 4. Data Retention & Deletion Rights
* Users may request the deletion of any stored data associated with their Discord account by contacting the server administration team.
* Server administrators may remove all configured form data at any time using the `/edit-form` command or by kicking/banning the Bot.

---

### 5. Third-Party Services
The Bot operates on the Discord platform and relies on Discord APIs. For more information on Discord's data practices, please review the [Discord Privacy Policy](https://discord.com/privacy).

---

### 6. Contact
If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please reach out to the Rass Derb Cup management team via the official Discord server.
